BranchDrive: Action-Conditioned Prediction & Selection

Manuscript · 2026

Co-developed a benchmark with 606 audited branch groups and 7,878 trajectories, pairing each canonical driving history with 13 simulator-executed futures.

History-plus-action prediction achieved 2.20 m test ADE versus 4.24 m for history-only; outcome-based selection reduced normalized regret from 0.267 to 0.150 in fixed-bank offline evaluation.

Auxiliary State Prediction for Driving Action Selection

Submitted manuscript · 2026

Compared joint state/outcome supervision with outcome-only training using an architecture-matched CNN-GRU, paired initialization, and a common validation outcome-loss checkpoint criterion.

On the primary 16-plan bank, outcome-only training achieved 2.897 m greater mean selected progress, with no selected collisions observed in either condition.

IEEE ICMLA 2026 · First author

Developed reward-free world-model pretraining using latent-ensemble disagreement, reaching 73.2% zero-shot success in an unseen town and, separately, 94.8% collision avoidance after limited adaptation.
